-38%

+44%

91%
Cognitive Comfort

The Challenge & Context

Increasing touchscreen screen sizes in modern vehicle dashboards cause dangerous cognitive overload, driver distraction, and interaction friction under chaotic Indian driving traffic.

User Research & Insights

Conducted in-vehicle ethnographic drive-alongs, eye-tracking gaze analysis, and cognitive load tests across city commutes, monsoon conditions, and nighttime highway drives.

UX Strategy & System Design

Developed an ergonomic HMI framework: prioritized glanceable tactile micro-gestures, dynamic contextual widgets that adjust to speed, and calm visual typography minimizing cognitive distraction.

Measurable Outcomes

Validated through road trials: eyes-off-road duration dropped by 38%, critical climate/media task completion speed improved by 44%, and drivers reported significantly higher spatial confidence.

The Challenge & Context

Extending Svaroots' modular shoe customization into augmented reality, allowing consumers to explore, scale, and test bespoke footwear on their own feet in real space before purchasing.

User Research & Insights

Identified that footwear buyers hesitate with unconventional modular configurations without seeing spatial proportions, surface texture lighting, and realistic scale on their own feet.

UX Strategy & System Design

Architected a lightweight WebAR try-before-you-buy feature with frictionless camera permissions, real-time colorway swapping, and modular sole configuration synced with the campaign catalog.

Measurable Outcomes

Boosted pre-order confidence, increased campaign customization sessions by 42%, and established an engaging bridge between digital configurators and physical product delivery.
